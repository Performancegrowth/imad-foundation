# IMAD — 3-Minute Demo Script

## Recording setup
- Windows: Win+Alt+R (Xbox Game Bar) or OBS Studio (free)
- Screen 1920×1080, browser maximised, notifications off
- Mic: headset or quiet room

## Scene 1 — Landing (0:00–0:20)
Show: http://localhost:8080
Say: "This is IMAD — an autonomous structural engineering engine. It takes a building description and produces a complete structural design in minutes."
Hover the hero image to reveal the structural frame.

## Scene 2 — Create Plan (0:20–1:00)
Action: Create Plan → Description tab
Type: "Two-story villa, 420 square meters total, 210 per floor, garage on north"
Click: Analyze Description
Show: Evidence panel with chips (420 sqm, 2 floors, residential)
Say: "IMAD extracts evidence and shows its reasoning. Every number is traceable."
Click: Generate. Show 3-step progress.
Show: Review panel with PASS/CHECK badges.
Say: "16 columns, 500mm beam depth, no columns in the living room — all verified."
Click: Save Plan.

## Scene 3 — Analyze (1:00–1:40)
Action: Click Analyze
Show: 3D viewer with load-coloured members
Say: "Analysis runs in seconds. Green is safe, red is near capacity. Let me click a beam."
Click: any beam
Show: Inspector panel — moment, shear, capacity, section, rebar, governing combo
Say: "245 kNm moment, 86% utilisation, 4Ø25 rebar, governed by 1.2D + 1.6L."
Show: SBC 304 compliance — 13 checks, 9 passed, 2 warned, 2 failed.
Say: "The design fails two SBC 304 checks. IMAD flags them immediately."

## Scene 4 — BOQ + carbon (1:40–2:20)
Action: Click BOQ & BBS
Say: "$63,000 total, $151 per square metre, 11,362 kg of rebar with 2.19% cutting waste."
Action: Click Sustainability
Say: "63.5 tonnes CO₂e — 'best practice' territory. Green alternatives: GGBS saves 40%, recycled steel 63%."

## Scene 5 — 3D + governance + review (2:20–2:50)
Action: Click 3D Model, orbit
Say: "The 3D model is the actual designed structure — not a sketch."
Action: Click Governance
Show: Submission readiness checklist
Say: "Compliance status, submission package, audit trail."
Action: Click Review & Sign
Say: "The signed package is ready for submission."

## Scene 6 — Close (2:50–3:00)
Say: "IMAD — from description to permit-ready design in minutes."
